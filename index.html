Rooms & team access Core

A “room” is one workspace for a brand/account’s content.

Create and manage rooms

Open Haiku, create a room, open settings, manage members, archive old rooms, or duplicate a room setup.

Built over Feb → Sep 17

Invite people the right way

Invites go out by email/notification. People accept or decline before they become members. Pending invites show clearly in the members list.

Shipped 21 Sep 2026

Connect social accounts

Connect Instagram (and more) from Connect Accounts / room settings — live data, not placeholders.

Shipped 3 Sep 2026

Planning the work Create & edit

Stage-based post editor

Content moves through clear stages: Idea → In Progress → Pending → Approved → Published/Closed. The form only shows what you need at that stage.

Redesigned early Sep 2026

Approve is not the same as publish

Someone can approve creative without publishing. Scheduling and “Publish” live on the Approved stage.

Shipped early Sep 2026

Idea notes & Ask Flo

Jot ideas first. Ask Flo to expand an idea into better copy. Save reusable Flo prompts.

Prompts 17 Sep · Ask Flo on Idea 23 Sep

Media & Google Drive

Upload media straight to cloud storage. Pull files from Google Drive. Media tab upload adds files onto a card properly.

Uploads 1 Sep · Drive 3 & 21 Sep · Media tab 23 Sep

Assignees & checklists

Assign people on a card. Track a checklist with progress on the board.

Shipped 20 Sep 2026

Guests can read, not break things

Guests see a clear “viewing only” experience — they can look and chat, not edit or publish.

Polished 23 Sep 2026

Board & calendar Day-to-day

Kanban board

Drag cards between columns. Cards show thumbnail, labels, project, due date, idea snippet, and schedule/publishing status.

Visual refresh early Sep · richer cards 22–23 Sep

Calendar & preview

See scheduled work on a calendar and preview how posts look before they go out.

Part of the room tabs (ongoing)

Workrooms (notes boards)

Need a simple task/notes board without social publishing? Create a Workroom — same Kanban idea, without Feed/Inbox/Publish clutter.

Shipped 22 Sep 2026

Table (spreadsheet view) 22 Sep

Manage many posts at once

Sort and group like a spreadsheet. Multi-select to change status, project, schedule, assignees, or archive in one go — built for agency ops.

Shipped 22 Sep 2026

Custom columns

Admins can rename/reorder room stages. Brands can save status templates and apply them when creating a new room.

Shipped 22 Sep 2026

Projects / campaigns 21–24 Sep

Group cards into campaigns

Create projects like “Summer Glow” or “UGC Collabs”. Filter the board by project. Cards still show under “All”.

Shipped 21 Sep 2026

Create into the project you’re viewing

If you’re filtered to a project, new cards start in that project.

Shipped 23 Sep 2026

Compact or wide project bar

Choose a slim filter strip or a larger card-style bar. Preference is remembered; switching animates smoothly.

Shipped 24 Sep 2026

Flo (AI helper) 19–23 Sep

Room Flo hub

Chat with Flo, set a room voice/persona, and run workflows — all inside the room’s Flo tab.

Shipped 19 Sep 2026

From chat idea → board card

After Flo brainstorms ideas, you can put them on the Kanban board with one confirm (or pick from a short list).

Shipped 21 Sep 2026

Scheduled & event workflows

Examples: weekly insights, fill calendar gaps, trend ideas. Also “when something happens” — e.g. email a share link when a card hits Pending, or nudge stale pending work.

Scheduled 19 Sep · events 23 Sep
Trend ideas are smart suggestions from room voice — not live web search of TikTok/Instagram trends.

Working with clients 17–21 Sep

Public share link

Send a link so a client can approve or comment without logging into Flit. Managers can get email when they act. Guests on the share link can upload media when allowed.

Approve/comment 17 Sep · uploads & emails 20 Sep

Collaboration review (accept / reject / changes)

Brand–creator collab review is separate from the content pipeline status — clearer for both sides.

UI 17 Sep · backend split 21 Sep

Weekly digest emails

Optional stakeholder digests from room settings — keep clients in the loop without a meeting.

Shipped 20 Sep 2026

Publishing & platforms Ongoing

Instagram publishing & scheduling

Connect IG, schedule or publish, see progress on the card (“Publishing…”, countdown when scheduled).

Core through early Sep 2026

Threads & Facebook connect

You can connect these platforms in the UI. Full live publish is still rolling out (connect works; publishing is stubbed until Meta Graph is fully live).

Connect UI 23 Sep 2026

One account per social room

By design: each Social room focuses on one connected account — keeps ops simple and reliable.

Product rule locked 22 Sep
TikTok shows as “Coming soon” — not connectable yet.

Analytics & reports Sep

Room Analytics

Instagram performance in-room. LinkedIn charts exist but need an extra LinkedIn permission for full data. Flo can add a short insights blurb.

IG Analytics 1 Sep · Flo insights 16 Sep

Client Report Packs

Managers generate a frozen report for 7 / 30 / 90 days (optionally scoped to one project). Copy a public link, or download CSV/PDF — no more screenshot decks.

Shipped 23 Sep 2026

Activity & card history

Room Activity tab shows what changed. Opening a card’s History shows before → after on fields (caption, status, project, due date, and more).

Activity tab 17 Sep · History rail 23 Sep

Instagram comments inbox 23 Sep

Moderate comments in Haiku

Social rooms get an Inbox tab: see comments, reply, hide/unhide, delete, or turn comments off on a post. Feed also merges organic Instagram posts with what you published from Haiku.

Shipped 23 Sep 2026
Instagram DMs are not in the product UI yet (only prepared for Meta app review). Production inbox also needs Meta “Advanced Access” turned on.

Who can do what Roles

Simple floors (higher roles include lower ones’ read access).

Guest

Look around, chat. No editing or publishing.

Creator

Create cards, upload, talk to Flo.

Editor

Edit others’ work, move status, publish, moderate inbox, bulk edits.

Manager

Projects, digests, Flo settings/workflows, report packs, integrations.

Admin

Customize status columns and room admin.

Not ready yet Be honest in demos

Don’t promise these in a client pitch

• Instagram Direct Messages inside Haiku
• Fully live Threads/Facebook publishing (connect is ahead of publish)
• TikTok connect
• Multi-account publishing inside one Social room
• Live web “what’s trending on IG/TikTok right now”
• Report packs for Workrooms (Social rooms only)
